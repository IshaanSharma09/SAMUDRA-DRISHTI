Conditions governing access to and use of the Samudra Drishti System

Effective Date: 1 September 2026
Version: 1.0
Issued by: INCOIS, Ministry of Earth Sciences

Access to this system is restricted to authorised personnel of the Government of India. Unauthorised access constitutes an offence under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (Sections 43 and 66) and may attract criminal prosecution.

1. Purpose and Scope

These Terms and Conditions govern the access and use of the Samudra Drishti National Maritime Surveillance System ("the System"), a classified operational platform operated by the National Institute of Ocean Information Services (INCOIS), Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India.

By accessing this System, the user acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms in their entirety. If you do not agree, you must discontinue access immediately and notify your Reporting Officer.

2. Authorised Access Only

Access to the Samudra Drishti System is granted exclusively to:

  • Designated personnel of INCOIS holding valid system credentials.
  • Personnel of partner agencies granted access by written authorisation from the INCOIS Programme Director.
  • Technical staff of the National Informatics Centre (NIC) for the purpose of infrastructure maintenance, under approved Change Management procedures.

Access is non-transferable. Credentials must not be shared with any other person under any circumstances.

3. Classification and Handling

All data accessible through this System is classified RESTRICTED under Government of India data classification standards. Users are responsible for:

  • Handling all system outputs in accordance with applicable classification handling instructions.
  • Not reproducing, distributing, or disclosing system data to any unauthorised person or system.
  • Not capturing screenshots, screen recordings, or physical photographs of classified data displayed on screen, except as explicitly authorised for official purposes.
  • Reporting any actual or suspected unauthorised access or data breach immediately to the INCOIS Information Security Officer.

4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited and may constitute offences under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Official Secrets Act, 1923:

  • Attempting to access the System without valid authorisation.
  • Attempting to probe, scan, or test the security of the System or its underlying infrastructure.
  • Introducing malware, viruses, or any malicious code into the System or connected networks.
  • Using the System for any purpose other than official maritime surveillance duties.
  • Circumventing or disabling any security control, access restriction, or audit mechanism.
  • Sharing, publishing, or transmitting any system output or classified data through unofficial channels.

5. Operational Accuracy and Disclaimer

The Samudra Drishti System provides AI-assisted analysis to support, not replace, human decision-making. All outputs — including spill detections, vessel suspicion scores, and satellite scheduling — are indicative and subject to human verification before any enforcement or operational action is taken.

INCOIS and the Government of India make no warranty, express or implied, regarding the completeness, accuracy, or timeliness of any data generated by this System. The System is provided in its current operational state and may be unavailable during scheduled or unscheduled maintenance windows.

6. Audit and Monitoring

All activity on this System is subject to continuous audit and monitoring. Access logs, session records, and usage data may be reviewed by authorised security personnel at any time without prior notice. Use of this System constitutes consent to such monitoring.
