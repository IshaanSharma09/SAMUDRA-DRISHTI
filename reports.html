How Reports Are Generated

  1. SAR Imagery Received — Sentinel-1 SAR scene is pulled from the ESA Copernicus Open Access Hub upon confirmed pass.
  2. AI Spill Detection — The onboard SDD-ResNet model classifies candidate oil slicks vs. meteorological look-alikes.
  3. Hindcast Correlation — Ocean drift modelling (INCOIS MOM6) traces the slick back to its probable source origin and time window.
  4. AIS Cross-Reference — Vessels present near the origin point within the correlated time window are ranked by suspicion score.
  5. Report Entry — Confirmed candidates above the detection threshold appear in this table for authorised operator review and action.
