Privacy Policy

Samudra Drishti National Maritime Surveillance System

Effective Date: 1 September 2026
Version: 1.0
Maintained by: INCOIS / NIC

This portal is classified RESTRICTED. Access is limited to authorised personnel of the Government of India. This privacy policy governs all data handling within the Samudra Drishti System web interface.

1. Preamble

The Samudra Drishti System is operated by the National Institute of Ocean Information Services (INCOIS), under the Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India, and is hosted on infrastructure maintained by the National Informatics Centre (NIC). This Privacy Policy describes the limited data handling practices of this portal.

This portal does not serve the general public. It is an internal operational tool for designated surveillance personnel. The practices described herein are intended to be transparent to authorised users regarding what data, if any, this interface handles.

2. Information This Portal Collects

2.1 No Personal Data Collected

This portal does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) from users. There are no user accounts, registration forms, contact forms, or analytics trackers embedded in this interface.

2.2 Theme Preference (localStorage)

A single item, sd-theme, is stored in your browser's localStorage to remember your light/dark mode preference. This value contains only the string "dark" or "light". It is never transmitted to any server and is stored exclusively in your browser. It is cleared when you clear your browser's site data.

2.3 Server Access Logs

Standard web server access logs maintained by NIC may record your IP address, timestamp, and the page requested. These logs are handled in accordance with NIC's IT infrastructure policies and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

3. Maritime Surveillance Data

All operational data processed within this portal — including Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel positions, Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery analysis results, and ocean environmental data — is classified RESTRICTED under applicable Government of India data classification frameworks. This data is:

  • Not shared with any third party outside the authorised operational chain.
  • Retained only as long as operationally necessary, per INCOIS data retention schedules.
  • Accessed only by personnel with appropriate security clearance and a need-to-know.

4. Third-Party Services Used by This Portal

This portal loads resources from the following external services. These services may have their own privacy practices.

  • Google Fonts — Loads the Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com. Google's privacy policy applies to font serving.
  • Google Translate — The optional language translation widget connects to translate.google.com. Page text may be transmitted to Google's servers when a translation is requested. Users are advised to disable this widget if handling sensitive operational data.
  • CartoDB / CARTO — Map tile images for the Live Map page are served from basemaps.cartocdn.com. CARTO's privacy policy applies to tile requests.
  • jsDelivr — The Leaflet.js library is loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net. jsDelivr's privacy policy applies.

None of these services are provided with any user PII by this portal.

5. Cookies

This portal does not set any HTTP cookies. The single piece of browser storage used is localStorage for theme preference, as described in Section 2.2. Google Translate, if activated, may set its own cookies. Disabling JavaScript will prevent the translate widget from loading.

6. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy and all matters arising from it are governed by the laws of the Republic of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules thereunder. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of competent courts in Hyderabad, Telangana.

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the Samudra Drishti Programme Office at INCOIS, Hyderabad. Official correspondence should reference the Samudra Drishti System and be marked to the attention of the Programme Director.

Address: National Institute of Ocean Information Services (INCOIS), Ocean Valley, Pragathi Nagar (BO), Nizampet (SO), Hyderabad — 500 090.

8. Amendments

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in operational practices or applicable law. The effective date at the top of this document reflects the date of the most recent revision. Authorised users will be notified of material changes through official INCOIS communication channels.
